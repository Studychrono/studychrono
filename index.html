Everyday help

Keep moving forward.

01Tasks & your study queue+

Tap + in Up Next to add a task. A title is required; class and due date are optional. Review the values, then tap Add. Use the task's action menu to edit local work.

Completing a local task moves it out of active work. Open Settings > Data > Completed Tasks to restore it or delete it permanently. Hide from Chrono on an imported Google item only hides it in this app; it does not complete or delete it in Google. Restore it in Settings > Data > Hidden Imported Items.

Suggested Start uses the available deadlines, task information and study history. Check any missing class or deadline rather than assuming an import filled it in.

02Connecting Google+

Use Connect Google from Up Next or Settings. Google handles sign-in and asks you to approve read-only Tasks and Calendar access. Connecting is optional; you can add local tasks without it.

If work is missing, check the Google account you selected and refresh Up Next with a working connection. The current importer reads a limited set of incomplete tasks from your default Tasks list and upcoming events from your primary calendar, then filters events for study relevance. It is not a complete mirror of every task list or calendar.

A school administrator may restrict third-party apps. If consent is blocked or the app name looks wrong, cancel and contact support with the exact warning, redacting account details. Do not bypass a security warning.

Settings > Account > Disconnect Google removes the app's stored credentials when successful and clears the current imported queue. It does not erase study history, learned matches or hidden-item records, and it does not revoke Google's server-side grant. See disconnect and deletion details.

03Focus sessions+

Choose a task or class, review the duration, then tap Start Focus. Use Pause and Resume as needed. Minimizing returns to normal navigation without intentionally ending the current session; return to Focus for the timer.

Focus Settings controls session length, short and long breaks, long-break frequency, automatic breaks and automatic repeat. Check those settings if another phase begins automatically. Saved study sessions appear in Stats.

If the timer or history looks incorrect, report the settings, whether the app was backgrounded, the approximate active and paused times, and what you expected. Do not delete your history as a troubleshooting step unless you want it removed.

04Assignment imports+

From a supported sharing app, use the Share Sheet and choose Study Chrono. Review the draft before saving. If iOS cannot open Study Chrono directly, finish sharing and open the app manually to review the pending draft promptly.

Safari may supply only a URL, or a URL and page title. A saved link does not mean the instructions, class or deadline were imported. Copy the assignment details, use Paste assignment details in the draft when available, and review the extracted fields. Use Import Task List for an actual list of separate tasks.

Imports are parsed on the device; Study Chrono does not sign in to or scrape Canvas, Schoology or other school platforms. Keep the original assignment link, check explicit years and times, and enter missing information yourself. A page's To-Do Date is not automatically treated as its assignment deadline.

Something not right?

Send a useful bug report.

You do not need to share your coursework to describe a problem. A short, reproducible example is usually best.

  1. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and Study Chrono version from Settings.
  2. List the steps, what happened, and what you expected instead.
  3. Include the app language, text size or Focus settings if relevant.
  4. Optionally attach a redacted screenshot or a reviewed Beta Feedback report.

Keep personal information out. Never send passwords, verification codes, OAuth tokens, private school links containing access codes, or other students' information. Redact names, account details and coursework from screenshots.
