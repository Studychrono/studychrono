Privacy, explained.

What the app processes, why it is used, and the controls available to you.

This policy describes Study Chrono for iPhone and iPad, as well as this support website. "Study Chrono," "we" and "us" refer to the app's operator, reachable at studychrono.support@gmail.com. Google connection is optional; local task and study features do not require a separate Study Chrono account.

01. Information the app processes

Study Chrono processes information you enter or choose to import to organize schoolwork, suggest study priorities, run Focus sessions and show study history. This includes:

  • Task titles, optional classes and deadlines, creation and completion dates, and any saved source links.
  • Study-session class, focused minutes, session date, mood value and any recorded start/end times.
  • App language, Focus settings, custom classes, learned class matches and local feedback records.
  • Assignment text, URLs and page titles supplied through sharing or paste, and Google information described below.

The app's class matching and study suggestions run on the device. They do not send your assignments to a generative AI service.

02. Device storage and system copies

Tasks and saved sessions are kept in Apple-managed app storage using SwiftData. Preferences, class-learning records, hidden-item records and beta activity use local app preferences. The current Google queue is maintained in app memory; some derived records are saved as described below.

Study Chrono does not operate an app-content backend in the implementation described here. Apple device backups, restores and any system-managed synchronization enabled for the distributed build may create additional copies, depending on the release configuration and your settings. System network caches may separately retain responses. This does not establish that iCloud synchronization is enabled for your version, or promise that every copy is confined to one iPhone. Removing a local record does not establish that a separate system backup has been erased.

03. Optional Google Tasks and Calendar access

When you choose Connect Google, the app requests tasks.readonly and calendar.readonly. The current importer reads incomplete items from your default Google Tasks list and upcoming events from your primary Google Calendar, with a limited result count and study-relevance filtering. It extracts identifiers, task titles, due dates, event summaries and event start dates/times. API responses can include additional metadata beyond the fields the app uses.

The app uses this information to display your queue, associate work with classes, suggest what to study, and provide task context for Focus. It does not complete, edit or delete your Google Tasks or Calendar events. Hiding an imported item affects Study Chrono only.

Google API data and Limited Use

Google's API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, governs use of data obtained through Google APIs, including derived data. The implemented uses described here are confined to the app's visible study features. The app does not sell Google data or transmit it to advertising platforms, data brokers or AI-training services. There is no automatic developer upload of this data. If you deliberately share Google-derived details in a support request or feedback report, we receive only what you choose to send for that purpose. This description is not a claim of Google certification or completed OAuth verification.

04. Authorization credentials and security

Google handles your password and consent through the iOS web authentication session. Study Chrono receives authorization credentials, not your Google password. The app uses PKCE and validates the authorization response's state before exchanging its code. Requests to Google's authorization, token and API endpoints use HTTPS.

This version stores access tokens, refresh tokens when provided, and token expiry information in the iOS Keychain with an after-first-unlock, device-only accessibility setting. On upgrade, a legacy token copy in app preferences is removed after a verified Keychain migration; a failed migration can leave that legacy copy pending until migration succeeds or it is cleared. iOS and Google may maintain their own sign-in/browser state.

These protections reduce risk, but no device, storage system or transmission method is guaranteed secure. Protect your iPhone and never send support your password, verification codes or tokens.

05. Disconnecting, retention and deletion

Use Settings > Account > Disconnect Google. When credential deletion succeeds, the app clears its stored Google credentials and current in-memory imported queues. If deletion fails, the app reports a disconnect error; do not assume you have disconnected successfully.

Disconnect does not erase saved local tasks, study sessions, learned class matches, hidden-item records or beta activity. Hidden records can contain the imported title, external identifier, source, deadline and time hidden. Learned records can include title-to-class mappings, patterns and example titles. Study history and feedback counts may also derive from work imported from Google.

Disconnect does not call Google's token-revocation service. To withdraw the server-side grant as well, remove Study Chrono's access in your Google Account connections. Neither action deletes the original tasks or events in Google.

Use the controls separately for the data you want to remove. A storage or save failure can prevent changes from being saved, and not every bulk deletion failure is reported by the current app. Reopen the app and check the resulting state before assuming deletion completed:

  • Clear local tasks in Settings removes saved local tasks, including completed ones and their source links. Individual completed tasks also offer permanent deletion in Completed Tasks.
  • Clear study history removes saved study-session records.
  • Reset learned class matches removes saved title mappings, learned patterns and subject-profile examples.
  • Hidden Imported Items > Restore / Restore All removes the selected exclusion records. Connected Google items may then reappear on refresh.
  • Beta Feedback > Reset Beta Activity clears the local beta activity summary and recommendation-feedback records.

Records persist until removed with the relevant controls; disconnect is not a combined erase-all action. Settings, custom classes, a pending shared capture, system copies and Keychain data are separate categories. Deleting or offloading the app is not a substitute for a verified Keychain deletion or Google revocation. Local controls do not delete a support email or copies you previously shared elsewhere. Contact support about information you have sent to us; we cannot remotely erase your iPhone or Google's records.

06. Assignment sharing and clipboard capture

The Share Extension processes the text, URL and page title, when supplied, that you choose to share. Continuing saves one pending draft in the on-device App Group shared by the extension and main app. It is not sent to a Study Chrono server. A subsequent share can replace that pending draft.

The main app parses supplied text locally and opens a reviewable draft. A URL alone does not provide the assignment's full details; the app does not scrape authenticated school pages. Clipboard content is read when you choose a paste/import action; the app may first check whether text or a URL is available to show that action. It does not continuously read clipboard contents.

The handoff payload is removed when successfully consumed for review. Payloads older than 30 minutes are rejected and cleanup is attempted when the app checks them; there is no guaranteed background deletion at the 30-minute mark. If reading or cleanup fails, a pending file may remain for retry. Shared content can therefore exist temporarily before you save a task. Saved tasks retain their reviewed fields and source link. Opening a source link leaves the app for that site's own access and privacy practices.

07. Activity, analytics and diagnostics

The current app includes no advertising SDK, cross-app tracking system or third-party analytics uploader. It does record local beta activity: task/save/completion counts, study-start/completion milestones and timestamps, and recommendation feedback with source categories and deadline buckets. These records support the visible beta-feedback feature and are not automatically sent to the developer.

If you choose to share a Beta Report, you can preview it before selecting a destination. The report includes app version, language, coarse milestones and counts, feedback reason counts and any comments you type. Its generated fields do not include task titles, source links, Google tokens or raw assignment text; your own comments or attachments can still contain personal information.

iOS, Apple crash reporting and TestFlight may process diagnostics according to your settings and their terms. The Share Extension also logs operational failures through the system logging facility. This policy does not promise that system diagnostics or a report you choose to share contain no personal information.

08. Support email and voluntary sharing

If you email studychrono.support@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and attachments. We use what you provide to respond, investigate the reported problem and handle the request. The mailbox is hosted by Gmail, and email providers process delivery and storage under their own policies.

Send only what is needed, and redact student names, private coursework, account details and access-bearing links. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations, rather than for a fixed period promised here. You can contact the same address about access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us. Information you share to other destinations is also subject to those recipients' practices.

09. This support website

These static pages add no analytics, advertising, cookies, external fonts or tracking scripts, and contain no sign-in or contact form. An email link opens your chosen mail application. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes even if they are not signed in. See GitHub's privacy statement and GitHub Pages data collection. Other linked services have their own privacy practices.

10. Contact and policy updates

For questions about this policy or a privacy request, email studychrono.support@gmail.com. We will update this page and its effective date when practices change. Where a change requires renewed Google consent, it must be obtained before the new use begins.
